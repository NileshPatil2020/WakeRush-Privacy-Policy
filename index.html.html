WakeRush Privacy Policy

Effective date: 25 October 2026
Last updated: 25 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how NTP STUDIO ("we", "us", "our") handles information in the WakeRush alarm clock app for Android (the "App"). We have designed the App to keep your data on your device. This policy describes what is stored, why, who else may receive data, and the choices you have.

1. Summary

  • WakeRush does not require an account or sign-in.
  • Your alarms, statistics, profile and settings are stored locally on your device.
  • We do not sell your personal information.
  • The App shows advertisements provided by Google AdMob, which may process device identifiers and ad-related data as described in Section 4.
  • The App does not currently send your alarm, profile or statistics data to any AI service.

2. Information stored on your device

CategoryExamplesPurpose
AlarmsAlarm time, label, repeat days, difficulty, number of sums, selected ringtoneTo ring alarms at the times you set

ProfileDisplay name, emoji avatar or a photo you choose, titlesTo personalise your profile

Data is stored in the App's private storage on your device. It is removed when you clear the App's data or uninstall the App.

3. Photos you choose

If you set a profile photo, the App uses Android's photo picker. The App only receives the image you select. We do not access your photo library in the background.

4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob. AdMob and its partners may collect and use information such as:

  • Advertising identifiers and device information
  • Approximate location derived from your IP address
  • Information about ads you view or interact with

Depending on your settings and applicable law, ads may be personalised. You can control ad personalisation in your Android settings (Settings > Privacy > Ads) and by resetting or deleting your advertising ID. Learn more about how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. Google's privacy policy is at policies.google.com/privacy.

Where required by law (for example, for users in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland), we will ask for your consent before personalised advertising is shown, using Google's consent tools.

5. Analytics and crash reporting

The App is configured not to collect Firebase Analytics data. The App does not include a third-party crash-reporting service in the current version. If this changes, we will update this policy.

7. Alarm screen

When an alarm rings, the alarm screen stays in front until you solve the math sums or use the Skip option. The back button is disabled on this screen. The App does not use Android screen pinning and does not collect any information through this feature.

8. Data sharing

We do not sell or rent your personal information. We share data only:

  • With Google AdMob, as described in Section 4.
  • When required by law or to protect rights, safety or security.

9. Children

WakeRush is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the App, please contact us so we can help.

10. Data security

Your data is stored in the App's private storage, which other apps cannot read under normal Android protections. No account data is transmitted to our servers because the App has no account system. Advertising requests are sent over encrypted connections. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so we encourage you to keep your device protected with a screen lock.

11. Data retention and deletion

Local data remains on your device until you delete it. You can delete all WakeRush data by clearing the App's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the App. Data held by Google AdMob is governed by Google's policies.

12. Your choices and rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or restrict the processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. You can exercise these rights by contacting us at the address below. You can also manage ad personalisation in your Android settings.

13. International users

Google AdMob may process data in countries other than your own, in line with Google's policies. Where required, appropriate safeguards are applied.

14. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will be shown in the App or on the store listing.
